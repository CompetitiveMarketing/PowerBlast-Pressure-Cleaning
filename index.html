Questions

Before we blast off.

What surfaces can you pressure clean?

Pressure cleaning can be suitable for a wide range of surfaces including concrete, driveways, paths, walls, bricks and other hard surfaces. The correct cleaning method depends on the material and condition.

Do you handle commercial cleaning?

Yes. PowerBlast offers cleaning solutions for commercial, facility and other larger-scale environments. Contact the team with your requirements so the appropriate approach can be discussed.

Can you remove mould and surface build-up?

PowerBlast provides mould remediation and specialist cleaning solutions designed for surfaces affected by unwanted growth and heavy contamination.
